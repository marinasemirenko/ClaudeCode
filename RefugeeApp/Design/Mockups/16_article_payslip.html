⚠️ Кілька місць роботи
Якщо ти працюєш у двох місцях — loonheffingskorting можна застосувати тільки в ОДНОГО роботодавця. Попередь другого роботодавця, щоб він не застосовував знижку, інакше наприкінці року отримаєш рахунок від belastingdienst.
Мінімальна зарплата 2025
Для дорослих (21+): €14.06 за годину. Роботодавець не може платити менше — незалежно від договору. Перевірити поточну ставку: rijksoverheid.nl/minimumloon

Key terms
arrow_upward
Brutoloon
Gross salary
What is written in the contract. Before all deductions.
percent
Loonheffing
Income tax
Automatically withheld by the employer. Rate depends on income — usually 9–37% for lower salaries.
arrow_downward
Nettoloon
Net salary
What actually arrives in your account — gross minus all deductions.
beach_access
Vakantiegeld (8%)
Holiday pay
8% of annual gross salary. Paid either monthly or in one payment in May.
savings
Loonheffingskorting
Tax credit
Reduces the loonheffing amount. Apply at only ONE employer — otherwise you will have to pay extra at year end.
work
Arbeidskorting
Employment allowance
Automatic reduction for everyone who works. In 2025 — up to €5,685 per year. Applied when filing the tax return.
Example pay slip
Brutoloon (gross)
€2,200.00
— Loonheffing (tax)
−€390.00
— Pensioen (pension)
−€55.00
Nettoloon (net)
€1,755.00
Vakantiegeld 8% (reserve)
+€176.00
Illustrative example. Real figures depend on salary and deductions.
⚠️ Multiple jobs
If you work in two places — loonheffingskorting can only be applied at ONE employer. Tell your second employer not to apply the credit, otherwise at year end you will receive a bill from the belastingdienst.
Minimum wage 2025
For adults (21+): €14.06 per hour. An employer cannot pay less — regardless of the contract. Check the current rate: rijksoverheid.nl/minimumloon
