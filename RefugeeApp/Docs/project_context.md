# Project Context — Refugee Helper App

## About the Creator

**Name:** Marina  
**Age:** 26  
**Current location:** Netherlands (living here for 3.5 years)  
**Background:** Ukrainian refugee. Migration path: Ukraine → Hungary (1 month) → Germany (2 months) → Netherlands (3.5 years)

Marina has personal, first-hand experience with the refugee journey — migration, integration, navigating bureaucracy, finding housing, understanding laws in a foreign country. This lived experience is the foundation of the project.

---

## Project Idea

A mobile app that serves as the **ultimate guide for Ukrainian refugees** — a comprehensive, structured reference tool covering everything a refugee needs to know when arriving in a new country.

The app is built by a refugee, for refugees.

---

## Target Audience

- Ukrainian refugees (primary focus)
- Newly arrived refugees who don't know where to start
- Refugees who have been abroad for a while but still have unanswered questions

---

## Core Features (Categories)

The app will be organized into clear categories:

1. **Laws & Rights** — legal information relevant to refugees (residence permits, status, rights)
2. **Healthcare** — how to access medical care, insurance, pharmacies
3. **Education** — schools for children, language courses, university recognition
4. **Work** — how to find a job, work permits, CV tips
5. **Housing** — how to find accommodation, tenant rights
6. **Useful Links** — curated list of trusted resources, organizations, hotlines
7. **Nuances & Tips** — practical day-to-day advice that's hard to find elsewhere

The goal is to cover **everything a refugee might need** — a one-stop reference guide.

---

## Vision

Marina wants to create the most complete and helpful guide for refugees that exists. The app should be:

- Easy to use, even for people who are stressed or overwhelmed
- Available in Ukrainian and the language of the host country
- Updated regularly as laws and resources change
- Something refugees use time and time again, not just once

---

## Monetization Plan

- **Subscription model:** ~€5/month for full access
- **Freemium:** basic content free, premium content behind subscription (to attract initial users)
- **Advertising:** add relevant ads once the app has a user base
- Long-term: partnerships with NGOs, organizations helping refugees

---

## Instructions for AI assistants

When helping with this project:
- Always keep in mind that the creator is a non-technical founder with a strong personal motivation
- The app should feel warm, human, and trustworthy — not cold or bureaucratic
- Prioritize simplicity and clarity in both UX and code
- The primary language of the app content is **Ukrainian**, with translations
- Suggest practical, achievable next steps — not overwhelming plans
